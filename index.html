About

Visualizing complex flows from rheology to interfaces.

Dr. Ravi Prakash is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology Agartala and leads the RAVI Lab (Rheology and Advanced Visualization of Interfaces). His research combines rheology, advanced flow visualization, experimental fluid mechanics, computational fluid dynamics and multiphysics simulations to study transport phenomena in confined and interfacial systems.

His work spans liquid–liquid and gas–liquid flows, wettability-controlled hydrodynamics, thin-film formation, miniature reactors, wave–structure interaction and, more recently, porous-media transport and hollow microneedle systems for transdermal drug delivery.

Research

Research themes

A connected program in rheology, interface dynamics, advanced visualization, miniaturized flow systems, computational modelling, and emerging biomedical applications.

01

Rheology & Advanced Flow Visualization

Characterization and visualization of complex-flow behaviour using rheological measurements, high-speed imaging and image-based analysis to resolve transient, confined and interfacial transport phenomena.

RheologyHigh-speed imagingFlow visualization
02

Multiphase Flow & Interfacial Transport

Liquid–liquid and gas–liquid hydrodynamics, slug flow, thin-film formation, wettability transitions, mass transfer, pressure drop and interface dynamics.

Slug flowWettabilityMass transfer
03

CFD & Computational Multiphysics

Finite-volume and finite-element modelling of multiphase flow, interface capturing, fluid–structure interaction, porous media and coupled transport phenomena.

VOFFSICOMSOL
04

Microfluidics & Process Intensification

Mini- and millifluidic reactors, serpentine and helical geometries, secondary-flow enhancement, extraction, double emulsions and intensified transport processes.

MicroreactorsHelical coilsExtraction
05

Biomedical Transport & Drug Delivery

Hollow microneedles, transdermal drug delivery, free-flow/porous-interface coupling, pharmacokinetics, fluid–structure interaction and Bayesian optimization.

MicroneedlesPorous mediaOptimization
